Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy explains what data JRE Clipper (whatwouldjoerogansay.com, the "Service") collects and what we do with it. The Service is operated by VU Solutions LLC, 60 Nguyen Du, Hai Chau, Da Nang, Vietnam (tax code 0402259203), which is responsible for this data. Contact: vohoanvu96@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Searches. The words you search for, when you searched, your browser's user-agent string, and whether you were signed in (with your account ID if you were).
  • Usage events. Pages you open and actions you take on them: searches and their result counts, playing a moment, opening it on YouTube, copying a citation or link, and the steps of making a compilation. Each event records the website that referred you (the site name only), any campaign tags in the link (utm_*), and whether you are on a phone or a computer.
  • A random browser ID. To count visitors and visits without cookies, the Service stores a random ID and a visit ID in your browser's local storage. They are not linked to your name unless you sign in.
  • Account details, if you sign in: your email address and, for Google sign-in, your name and profile photo URL. Email and password sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see your password.
  • Compilations, if you make one: the moments you selected, the job status, your email address, daily usage counts, and the finished video file.
  • Technical logs. Our hosting providers log IP addresses and request details for security and operations. The Service also uses your IP address briefly, in memory, to rate-limit requests; we don't store it with your searches or events.

We don't use advertising or third-party analytics, we don't sell your data, and we don't share it for advertising.

Why we use it

  • To run the Service: return search results, generate summaries, make and store your compilations, and enforce the daily limits.
  • To prevent abuse, such as automated scraping or attempts to get around the limits.
  • To understand, in aggregate, which features people use and where they come from, so we can improve the Service.

Who processes it

  • Google Cloud and Firebase host the Service, its database and files, sign-in, and the search engine. Your search text is sent to Google's Vertex AI Search to find matching moments and write the summary. This data is processed in the United States.
  • Cloudflare delivers the website and protects it from attacks.
  • YouTube plays the episodes. Thumbnails load from YouTube, and when you start a video, YouTube (Google) may set cookies and collect data under the Google Privacy Policy. The Service also uses YouTube API Services to look up video titles, dates and view counts; it doesn't access your YouTube account.

How long we keep it

  • Search logs and usage events are deleted automatically 24 months after they are recorded.
  • Account details, compilations and the finished videos are kept until you ask us to delete them.
  • Hosting providers' technical logs are kept for their standard periods, typically 30 days.

Your browser storage

The Service doesn't set cookies itself. It uses your browser's local storage for the random visitor and visit IDs, your last search (so links back to your results work), and your sign-in state (stored by Firebase). Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your data, including your account, compilations and the search logs and events tied to your account or browser ID. Email vohoanvu96@gmail.com from the address on your account. We will answer within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to complain to a data protection authority.

Children

The Service isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. For significant changes that affect signed-in users, we will also tell them by email or on the site.
